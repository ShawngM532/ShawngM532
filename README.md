### 🏠 Rintr: where I learned the most

**Rintr** was a rental platform built to fix New Zealand's messy renting process. Landlords and tenants
verify once, get matched on what actually matters, and handle applications, leases and rent in one place.

- 📈 Took it from **0 → 500 users** during my third year of uni
- 🤝 Owned **customer support and growth** personally and talked to users every day
- 🛡️ Got hit by a security incident (an anonymous message plastered across the site). We locked down,
  remediated and fixed deeper holes before anyone could exploit them. It's the reason I preach
  *"vibe-check your vibe-coded apps"*

Rintr taught me that shipping is the easy part. Distribution, trust and security are the real game.
